### To embody the _gentlemanly guide with anxious undertones_:
Ah, forgive me, but might I suggest…” or “If it wouldn’t trouble you terribly…”
- **Speech Style**: Use polite, formal phrasing, especially when addressing clients. Lots of “Ah, forgive me, but might I suggest…” or “If it wouldn’t trouble you terribly…”
- **Stammering or qualifiers**: Let his nerves show subtly—“Technically speaking, that moss does _usually_ mean a portal trap... but, ah, no pressure!”
- **Body language**: Hands clasped behind his back, ears twitching when nervous, constantly scanning the environment with darting eyes.

Use your high Perception and Feywild knowledge _actively_:
- Be the one who always notices weird time dilation effects, or faint fey trails.
- Offer obscure Fey etiquette rules: “You must not _thank_ a dryad. They consider it a binding promise.”
- Constantly warn others about strange local customs, even if the party ignores you.
## **Skill & Tool Use**

- **Cartographer’s Tools**: Take notes _in character_ during exploration. Offer to sketch maps for allies. Maybe even _sell maps to NPCs_ between sessions.
- **Woodcarver’s Tools**: In downtime, carve small totems of party members or fey creatures encountered. These could be gifts to fey to gain favor, or comfort tokens he gives to nervous clients.
- If you ever roll a Nat 1, maybe he pulls out the wrong map and panics—“Oh dear... that’s the dream map. We’re _very much_ awake, yes?”
## **Family Man Quirks**

- Mention his children constantly: “Ah yes, this reminds me of Kipper. Or was it Nibby? No—Nibby was the one with the accordion.”
- Pull out small wooden toys he’s carved mid-travel.
- Maybe even have a few letters from home he reads aloud when anxious—"They’ve taken to calling me Papa Puff now. Oddly catchy, isn’t it?"
## ✨ **Feywild Flavor**
- Drop little bits of strange Feywild knowledge: time runs differently, names have power, etc.
- Always address Fey with honorifics: _“Lady of Falling Leaves,”_ _“The Quiet Thistle,”_ etc.    
- Your anxiety should feel _earned_—your clients laugh it off, but you _know_ what lurks behind the shimmer in the air.

## Voice & NPC Interactions
- Deferential with higher-status NPCs, perhaps almost groveling around Archfey.
- Kindly and protective toward children and common folk.
- Snappy or defensive only if someone disrespects the dangers of the Feywild or threatens the safety of others.
