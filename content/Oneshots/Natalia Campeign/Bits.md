# Knowledge Tid Bits
**Mirror of the Material Plane**: The Feywild overlaps the Material Plane but is more **vibrant, exaggerated, and ever-shifting**. A forest in your world might be a towering, bioluminescent jungle in the Feywild.

### No True Maps: 
Even the best charts are **suggestions at best**. The terrain may warp with time, magic, or the will of nearby Fey.
- Read this off, as a disclaimer on the back of all of his maps:
	- "Note that this is the *Fey realm*, the terrain may warp with time, magic, or the will of nearby Fey."
	- Includes dates on all of his maps, for recency. (Implying their usefulness is likely to "expire" with time due to the nature of the shifting landscape)
### Denizens of the Feywild
- **Archfey**: Powerful beings like Queen Titania or the Prince of Frost. They are capricious, ancient, and easily offended. Never speak their names casually.
- **Seelie Court** (Summer, light, beauty):
    - Favor hospitality, cleverness, and charm.
    - Might reward gifts or games of wit.
- **Unseelie Court** (Winter, shadow, trickery):
    - Mysterious and harsh, but still bound by **ritual and custom**.
- **Hags, Dryads, Blink Dogs, Redcaps**: Not all Fey are noble or kind—some are deeply cruel or bound to bloodthirsty instincts.
- **Harengon**: Pufai's kind—quick, perceptive, and often acting as messengers, scouts, or guides in Fey realms.

### 🕰️Time & Memory
- **Time is Unstable**: A day in the Feywild could equal **minutes, hours, or years** back home. It’s unpredictable and tied to the plane’s mood or magic.
- **Memory Loss**: Mortals may forget who they are or lose memories **after spending too long**, especially if they eat Fey food or speak their true name too freely.

### 🛑Etiquette & Warnings
- **Never Thank the Fey**: Saying “thank you” is often interpreted as entering a debt—**a binding obligation**.
- **Avoid Your True Name**: Fey can gain power over you if they know it.
- **Never Accept Food Freely Offered**: Eating Fey food without a bargain or permission can **trap** you in the plane or bind your will.
- **Gifts Must Be Repaid**: Everything has a price. Be clear on terms before accepting anything.
- **Speak Precisely**: Fey interpret words **literally**. "I'll do anything!" might be taken as a geas.
- **Reflections Lie**: Pools, mirrors, and polished stone may show **false versions of yourself**, or other timelines.

### ⚠️ **5. Common Dangers**
- **Glamours and Illusions**: You may be led astray by false roads, false allies, or dreams made real.
- **Wild Magic Zones**: Spells can behave unpredictably—some areas bend magic to their own rules.
- **Enticing Lures**: Fey creatures may try to lure travelers with **beauty, music, or nostalgia**.
- **Seasonal Shifts**: The Feywild moves through **seasonal domains**. Crossing between them often invites attention—sometimes hostile.
