
# Party Interactions

- “Ah—greetings! Pufai’s the name. Guide, mapmaker, anxious wreck… though hopefully not in that order.”
- “Yes, yes, I’m familiar with the route. No, I’m not comfortable with it. That’s _why_ I’m good.”
- “You all look terribly capable, which is comforting. Terribly comforting. Hopefully comforting enough.”
- “Do you all have emergency moss? No? Hmm. Well—first rule of the Feywild: _assume you’ll need moss._”
	- **It might grow toward the nearest portal, memory, or heartbreak**, depending on the time of day.
	- “It _used_ to point north. Now it points toward regret, but at least we’re consistent.”
	- “The moss wouldn’t grow here if it weren’t _mostly_ safe. Unless it’s luring us, of course.”
		- Tap the moss to see if the land is listening. Try speaking to it.
- “I must confess—I _do_ carry a whistle, but I only use it in parental emergencies or if I spot a dancing goat.”

# Guiding
## 🗺️ **While Guiding Through the Feywild**

- “Ah—now don’t stray from the path. Last time someone did, they came back two feet taller and allergic to iron.”
- “That shimmer there? That’s not sunlight, dear—it’s _listening._”
- “If the trees start humming in harmony, we should respectfully _not_ hum along.”
- “You may feel an urge to dance. Kindly resist. Unless you want to dance _forever._”
- “Please, mind the flowers. They get terribly insulted when trampled and they do hold grudges.”

---

## ⚠️ **Warning of Danger**

- “That toadstool's got spots shaped like teeth—charming, yes, but absolutely carnivorous.”
- “Don’t make eye contact with the puddles. They remember.”
- “That path wasn’t here yesterday. Which is precisely why we shan’t take it.”
- “Do _not_ say your name out loud. Not unless you’d like to hear it spoken back _wrong._”
- “If you see a second version of me, do let me know. I haven’t licensed any duplicates this week.”

---

## 🧚 **Comments on Fey Beings**

- “They _seem_ delightful until they invite you to tea, and then it’s contracts and curses all the way down.”
- “Remember: if a faerie offers you a gift, the correct response is, ‘How _generous,_ but alas, I’m allergic.’”
- “That’s a sprite. Or a will-o’-wisp. Hard to say from this angle. Let’s not greet it just yet.”

---

## 🐰 **Fatherly or Home Life References**

- “My sixth-youngest, Tuffbit, once got stuck in a pixie ring. Had to bribe him out with jellyroot.”
- “Ah, this reminds me of our back garden—except _significantly more haunted._”
- “If you think this is chaotic, you’ve clearly never witnessed our burrow’s bedtime routine.”
- “My children are far less likely to trick you into an eternal sleep—but only marginally.”
- “One of my middle triplets once tried to trade our front door to a nymph. Charming negotiations, but we needed the door.”

## 💭 **Thoughtful or Wise Remarks**

- “The Feywild isn’t cruel, you see. It simply plays by rules we were never taught.”
- “Time is different here. It bends around stories, not hours. Best we mind our tale.”
- “A good map helps—but instinct’s better. The land listens more than it speaks.”
