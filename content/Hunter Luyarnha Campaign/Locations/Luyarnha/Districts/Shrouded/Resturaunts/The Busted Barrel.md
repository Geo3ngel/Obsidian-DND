## House Special
The house special is a shot of Wispy Sour from the previous owner’s private stash, which Planters tries to pass off as his own recipe, the common version is his recipe, the uncommon version is the original recipe. (Gain the benefits of the uncommon variant to Wispy Sour) [100 gp]

## Description
The Busted Barrel (originally called The Bustling Barrel): This humble tavern is run by a brown bearded, bald human man named Planters Fix, a fellow soldier with Grey during their time as basic foot soldiers for the western elven empire. After Grey was promoted, Planter staged an ambush with a partner who shot him in the knee, using the chance to retire from the military and move out of the empire. He took his funds to Luyarnha a couple years back and invested into a bar, working as the bouncer for The Busted Barrel. However, during a Hunt, the bar was attacked and Planters hid while the creatures laid waste to the establishment. The owner and his family were killed in the process, leaving Planters as the next in line for ownership of the bar.
