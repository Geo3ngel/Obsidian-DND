# Lore
The **1st and highest Rank** in the order of the Paladins of the Eldritch Hunt.
- Alternatively referred to as `Commander`
This Rank indicates the head of the organization, it's very leader.

### Commander/Master of the Hunt:
The executive decision maker of the legion and proof of his leadership being shown through a beautiful greatsword, wielded by their current Master of the Hunt, [[Ludwig]] Kroening.
