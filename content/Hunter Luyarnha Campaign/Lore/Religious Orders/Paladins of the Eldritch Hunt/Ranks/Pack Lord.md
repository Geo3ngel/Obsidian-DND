# Lore
The **2nd Rank** in the order of the Paladins of the Eldritch Hunt. Below only the Master of the Hunt himself.
- Alternatively referred to as `Captain`
Responsible for the tactical planning of their respective responsibilities within the order, with two Pack Lords always being chosen. 
- One specializes in defense, the other in offense. 
- 
The current Pack Lord for **defense** is [[Thokk]] Bloodbather who is responsible for choke points, blockade building, and reconnaissance/real time information gathering. 

The current Pack Lord for **offense** is [[Cona]] Organrender who is responsible for frontal assaults, flanking strikes, and hunting/assassination missions.
