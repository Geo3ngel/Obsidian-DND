# Lore
The **3rd Rank** in the order of the Paladins of the Eldritch Hunt.
- Alternatively referred to as a `Pack Leader` or `Lieutenant`
Paladins of this rank are effectively the leaders of groups of [[Stalker]]s (members) and [[Scout]]s (initiates).

The leaders of each smaller team that carries out orders from the their respective Pack Lord (3 for each pack lord, 6 in total).
