# Lore
The **4th Rank** in the order of the Paladins of the Eldritch Hunt.
- Alternatively referred to as a  `Member`

Officially recognized members who've fully taken the Oath of the Eldritch Hunt and completed proper initiation. They then choose which Pack Leader they'd like to follow if multiple leaders have taken an interest. (With notable members of course)

Members have access to better wares at [[The Silver Wolf]] than Scouts, having proven themselves as members of the order. (And to be worth investing in)

There are about ~30 Stalkers
