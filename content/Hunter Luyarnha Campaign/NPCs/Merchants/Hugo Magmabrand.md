# Description:
A somewhat young looking but gruff acting man with soot on his face.
He has an ample build and rustic charm to go with his trimmed beard and thick black hair matted from his work in the shop.

He has a passion for gunsmithing and works on modifying weaponry he’s given by other hunters to craft his own guns in his free time.
